Our Story

Proudly Serving Nairobi, Kenya Since 2024

URO-CARE Urology & Andrology Center has grown to become East Africa's foremost destination for specialist urological and andrological care for both men and women.

Founded in Nairobi, we established the practice based on three pillars: clinical excellence, absolute patient confidentiality, and a commitment to making world-class urological care accessible across Kenya and the region.

We combine advanced diagnostic technology with a deeply human approach — understanding that urological conditions often carry emotional and personal weight that demands sensitivity alongside expertise.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

A decade of growth, innovation and unwavering commitment to urological excellence in Nairobi Kenya and Eat Africa.

2024 — Foundation

Opened Nairobi's First Dedicated Urology Center

Established as the city's first dedicated urology & andrology specialist center offering comprehensive care for prostate disease, kidney stones and urinary tract disorders.

2025 — Expansion

Introduced Minimally Invasive Procedures

Pioneered advanced minimally invasive procedures: male infertility microsurgery, erectile dysfunction treatment programs, serving patients from across East Africa.

2025 — Technology

State-of-the-Art Diagnostics Installed

Invested in cutting-edge urodynamics equipment and digital diagnostics imaging, enabling same-day results and more accurate treatment planning than ever before.

2026 — Today

Growing Alongside Our Community

Continuing to grow alongside our community — expanding our specialist team and adding new clinical offerings to bring world-class urology care closer to every Kenyan.

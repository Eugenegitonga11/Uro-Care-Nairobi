About URO-CARE

Nairobi's Premier Urology & Andrology Center

Located at the 4th Floor, PMC, 3rd Parklands Avenue, URO-CARE has been delivering world-class specialist urological and andrological care to patients across Kenya and the East Africa region since 2024.

Our multidisciplinary team of fellowship-trained urologists, andrologists and support specialists provide evidence-based,compassionate and completely confidential care.

  • MOH-licensed facility with state-of-the-art diagnostic technology
  • In-house laboratory, pharmacy and imaging — all under one roof
  • 100% confidentiality for all men's health and andrology cases
  • Same-week specialist appointments — no referral required

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral to see a urologist at URO-CARE?

No referral is required. You can book directly by calling +254 112 288 709, WhatsApp, or our online booking form. We welcome self-referrals as well as GP referrals.

Is my consultation fully confidential?

Absolutely. All consultations and treatments at URO-CARE are completely confidential. We understand the sensitive nature of urological and andrological conditions.

What insurance providers do you accept?

We work with most major Kenyan insurance providers. Please contact us with your insurance details and we will confirm coverage before your appointment.

Where exactly is URO-CARE located?

We are at the 4th Floor, PMC Building, 3rd Parklands Avenue, Nairobi. Open Mon–Fri 9:00am–5:00pm and Saturday 10:00am–3:00pm.

Do you treat both men and women?

Yes. While we specialise in andrology (men's reproductive health), our urology department treats all patients — men, women and children — for conditions including kidney stones, bladder disorders and UTIs.

How long does a first consultation take?

First consultations typically take 45–60 minutes and include a detailed medical history, physical examination, and discussion of findings and next steps.

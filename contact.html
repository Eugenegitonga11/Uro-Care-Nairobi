Common Questions

Before You Visit

Everything you need to know about finding us, booking and your first visit.

Where exactly is URO-CARE located in Nairobi?

We are located at the 4th Floor, PMC Building, 3rd Parklands Avenue, Nairobi. We are open Monday–Friday 9:00am–5:00pm and Saturday 10:00am–3:00pm. Ample parking is available on-site.

How do I book an appointment?

Use our online form on this page, call +254 112 288 709, send a WhatsApp, or email info@urocare.co.ke. No referral is required and same-week appointments are usually available.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your national ID or passport, insurance card (if applicable), any previous medical reports or scan results, and a list of any current medications. Arriving 10 minutes early allows time for registration.

Is parking available at the clinic?

Yes. Ample parking is available at the PMC Building on 3rd Parklands Avenue. The clinic is also accessible via matatu routes serving the Parklands area.

Is my appointment completely confidential?

Yes, 100%. All consultations at URO-CARE — especially for erectile dysfunction, male infertility and other sensitive conditions — are treated with absolute discretion. Nothing is shared without your explicit written consent.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

We welcome walk-in patients subject to availability. However, we strongly recommend booking in advance to guarantee your preferred time and doctor. Call +254 112 288 709 or WhatsApp us first to check availability.
